אְסַר | ʔǝsar | to bind, take captive. verb.
דְחֵיק/דְחַק | dǝḥeq/dǝḥaq | to press, urge; to oppress (yāt, bǝ-, lǝ-). verb.
הְפַך | hǝpak | to overthrow; to convert, change (x into y: yāt x lǝ-y). verb.
טְמַר | ṭǝmar | to hide, conceal. verb.
תְבַר | tǝbar | to break, break down; to subdue. verb.
אַפַיָא | ʔappayyā | face, surface. noun. plural; most frequent in prep. phrases, esp. ʕal ʔappe (with pl. suffixes, §7.2) on the face/surface of; right up against, over against.
בָרָא | bārā | the outside (of a place). noun. used mainly in fixed prepositional and adverbial expressions: lǝ-bārā to the outside; mib-bārā on the outside (of: lǝ-); lǝ-mib-bārā to the outside (of: lǝ-); bār min except, except for, other than.
יְדָא | yǝdā | hand. noun. cstr. yad; pl. -ayyā; feminine; sing. with suffix: yǝdi, yǝdāk, yǝdeh, etc., but yadkon; note forms with preceding w, b, etc.: w-idā, b-idā, w-idayyā, b-idayyā, etc.; with prep. min: either miy-yǝdā or m-idā. bǝ-yad (or b-idā dǝ-) into the hand/power/control of; through, by means of; nǝseb b-ideh he picked up.
יוי/יי | Ywy/Yy | the divine name. noun. the usual writing; presumably read ʔǝdonāy as in Hebrew.
כַרמָא | karmā | vineyard. noun.
מַדבְחָא | madbǝḥā | altar. noun. cstr. madbaḥ; pl. -ayyā.
מַלכֻותָא | malḵutā | kingdom, reign, rule. noun. cstr. malḵut; pl. malḵǝwātā.
קְרָבָא | qǝrābā | battle, war. noun. pl. -ayyā; ʕǝbad qǝrābā ʕim to wage war against.
שְמָא | šǝmā | name. noun. cstr. šom or šem; pl. šǝmāhātā.
