קְדָם | qǝdām | before, in the presence of, prep. (with a suffix, the base is qǝdām-)
תְחֹות | tǝḥot | under, beneath, prep. with a suffix, the base is tǝḥot- (but 3rd fem. sg. is tǝḥot-ah).
בֵין | ben | between, prep. with a suffix, the base is ben- (but 1st pers. sg. is ben-a, sometimes ben-i).
עַל | ʕal | on, upon, prep. with a suffix, the base is ʕǝl- (but 3rd fem. sg. is ʕǝl-ah).
עִילָוֵי | ʕillāwe | on, upon, prep. with a suffix, the base is ʕillāw-; a synonym of ʕal, used especially in the compound me-ʕillāwe (= me-ʕal) when pronominal suffixes are required.

לֵילְיָא | lelǝyā | night. noun.
תַרעָא | tarʕā | gate (of a city), doorway (of a house or tent). noun.
אְרֵי | ʔǝre | (the fact) that; when, since, because. conj. corresponds to Hebrew kî.
כֹל | kol | all of, the whole of. noun. also written with a qametz in imitation of Tiberian. Before an emphatic singular noun used definitely: kol mešǝrā the whole plain; kol qartā the whole city; kol ʔarʕā the whole earth, all the land; kol qinyāneh all of his property. With a suffix the base is kull-: kulleh ‘all of it (m)’.
